I build Vol.

A lot of pilots I know are lonelier than they say. Finding people to fly with can be hard. Becoming friends and staying connected when you're not flying is harder. I want Vol to make both a little easier.

JP Summers and I started Vol in Verbier, Switzerland. He is my friend, flying partner, and cofounder. I am the only engineer trying to see how far one engineer can get with AI.

Vol

A flight log built around the people you fly with.

I built the codebase and the infrastructure from nothing: flight logs, 3D replay, maps, the feed, notifications, chat.

The track is not the point. Follows, stickers, comments, photos, messages. A place to stay in each other's days after you land.

How I build

One engineer. A lot of agents. A live product.

I still write code. I also run several agents at once. Cursor, cloud agents, Codex, Claude, SuperGrok, Amp Code. They implement, research, review, test, and debug. I decide what we are building, the shape of it, and what ships.

The interesting part is not the tool list. The tools will change. The interesting part is what happens when one person tries to operate a real web and mobile product this way, in production, with pilots using it.

I use machines so fewer humans are required to ship software. The software I'm building is about needing more humans in your life.
